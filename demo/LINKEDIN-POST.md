# LinkedIn post draft

I’ve been building **Orbit / J.A.R.V.I.S.**, a local-first personal AI assistant prototype.

The idea is simple: give the assistant an outcome, let the right specialist workflows coordinate, then review a concise result with a receipt. The showcase illustrates task routing, department views, approval boundaries, and a compact activity trail.

This is a portfolio prototype, not a claim of fully autonomous production capability. The interactive demo uses synthetic data; its task actions are simulated and it is not connected to personal accounts, devices, or the host machine.

I’m exploring how to make local AI workflows more useful, understandable, and verifiable in day-to-day work.
